# Dev-033: Cutaway for buildings and large terrain

[Rendering] [Bugfix] [Upgrade]

## Goal

Open Niko's storey when a roof or a building covers him, and cut the ground that hides him
(transparent, with its outer silhouette outlined and the cut closed by a rim), so the floor, stairs
and interior walls stay readable.

## Work

- [x] Give built floor and stair slabs their own face codes (`6`/`7`) so the cliff window, which
      only targets the natural-ground codes `0`/`1`/`2`, leaves them whole.
- [x] Keep a wall's face code on its top strip instead of forcing code `0`, so the window does not
      punch it either.
- [x] Cut by storey band, not by a flat plane: a slab whose floor band is above Niko's, or a wall,
      prop or furniture fragment in a higher band, is discarded. A plane at 3 m would leave the slab
      of the next floor showing from its underside.
- [x] Cut the walls of Niko's own band on the camera side (a north wall owns `y > niko.y`, a west
      wall owns `x > niko.x`) to a half-metre stub, so the two near walls open the room while the
      walls behind him stay whole.
- [x] Gate the band cut on `Cutaway.ClipH` (roofed, or a building between Niko and the camera). It
      reads only floors and walls, never natural terrain, so a cliff hides the tiles through the
      window without opening a building. `X`'s current-level view and `C`'s toggle stay.
- [x] Replace the fixed cliff circle with a line-of-sight hole (`Terrain.gdshader`): natural ground
      in front of Niko clears inside `Cutaway.CliffCutRadius` (`6` m) on both screen axes and only
      above `0.5` m over his feet, so the floor and the lower stairs keep their tiles. The hole keeps
      a thin rim at its boundary (`cliff_cut_radius` and the `0.5` m line), so the cut closes as a
      shape. A narrow `1.2` m column (too little clearance), a wide wedge (`0.18`), a band cut over
      an 8 m radius and a height-unbounded slot (a straight slit through the world) were tried and
      reverted: the heightmap draws only exposed faces, so a wide or unbounded cut shows the
      background through the hole.
- [x] Open the cliff window only on its own occluder: `Cutaway.TerrainHidden` (terrain-only) replaces
      the shared `Hidden` gate, so a building that covers Niko opens its storey by band and never
      cuts the ground around him. `CliffCutRadius` stays `6` m: shrinking it to `2.2` m and to Niko's
      body column read as a small rectangle and was reverted.
- [x] Give the cursor its own window, live only while the right button is held: `Cutaway.CursorTerrain`
      marches the mouse ray over the dump's solid tops in mesh space and, while it lands above Niko's
      half-metre line, centers a second hole there. It is fully round (no view-plane line through
      its center) and both areas fuse into one, the rim tracing the union's outer boundary with no
      seam where they overlap.
- [x] Cut for the camera only: the window `discard`s in the main pass (never `ALPHA = 0`, which kept
      depth and sent the terrain to the transparent pipeline) and skips the cut in the shadow pass
      (`IN_SHADOW_PASS`), so the removed mass keeps casting its shadow as if it were intact; the cut
      changes what is seen, not the lighting.
- [x] Keep full transparency. Ghosting the cut terrain (`blend_mix, depth_draw_always`, alpha `0.4`)
      was tried and rejected: a veil over Niko reads worse than a clean hole, and it forced the whole
      terrain material into the transparent pass. The discard rewrite dropped `ALPHA` and
      `depth_prepass_alpha` entirely: the material is opaque and writes no depth where it clears.
- [x] Draw the cut mass as a silhouette: `ChunkMesher` emits an `Outline` surface with a `0.06` m
      strip along each drawn ground face's top crest and along the ends it does not share with the
      next tile, and `WorldClient` builds an unshaded `_outlineMat` that draws them only inside the
      same hole (above `0.5` m over Niko's feet, in front). No internal grid lines. An ungated crest
      on all four drop directions was tried and reverted: it drew over walls and floors and left
      artifacts in the terrain.
- [x] Close the opening on all four sides: `Terrain.gdshader` draws the rim band with the same
      outline cream instead of the terrain colour, and the rim also covers the far cut on the view
      plane through Niko, so the hole reads as a traced contour (the north and west edges had no
      trace at all). `ChunkMesher` also emits a bare top crest for the north and west drops, whose
      risers face away from the camera and are never drawn, skipping any edge with a wall or a floor
      resting on the ground (the guards the earlier four-direction attempt lacked).
- [x] Probe along the real camera ray: `Cutaway.RayToCamera` feeds `Hidden`, `Occluded` and the
      shader's `cliff_cut_back`, and steps at `0.2` up to `72` along it, instead of the hardcoded
      1:1:1 walk.
- [x] Add `CompassOverlay` (`game.ui`, bottom-right of the GAME view): it draws the iso tile diamond
      with north, east, south and west on the screen directions the camera projects them to, plus
      `CAM` on the camera side, so a face can be named when reporting a cutaway problem.
- [x] Run `npm run check`.
- [x] Inspect the around-player and current-level shots for the test-world building and terrace from
      an isolated copy of the save; the original save is not modified. Around-player shots at
      x1/x2/x4 from isolated copies were inspected and the user accepted the cutaway live on
      28/09/2026.
- [x] Move this doc to `docs/done/` now that it is accepted, and add its Dev Blog entry plus the
      Systems Index note; the daily Work Report entry is the commit's tags and TL;DR, so it lands
      with the release commit.

## TL;DR

A building over or in front of Niko hides every built band above his, keeps his own storey's walls
and cuts the front ones to a stub. Natural ground above `0.5` m over his feet that hides him goes
transparent inside a bounded hole; the hole keeps a rim traced in the outline cream all around its
opening, and an outline pass draws the terrain's outer silhouette edges, so the floor and stairs
under him stay whole and the cut reads as a closed shape. A small iso compass in the HUD names the
faces on screen.
