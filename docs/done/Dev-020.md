# Dev-020: Wall thickness

[Feature] [Rendering]

Walls are drawn as zero-thickness planes on the tile edge, closed by a 1 px `line` mask on top.
On screen that top is 1, 2 or 4 px at x1, x2 and x4, so every wall reads as a sheet of paper. This
doc gives walls a visible body: a top strip, an end face where a run stops, and a corner post. It
is render-only. The wall stays on its edge for the server, physics, picking, occlusion and the
cutaway; nothing moves on the plane that is drawn today.

Reference: `docs/utils/VISION.md` [Sec. 3] (camera: 1 m tile = 64×32 px, 0.5 m = 16 px) and [Sec. 5]
(walls occupy tile edges); `CONTEXT.md` "Edge walls belong to the tile that owns the edge".

## 0. Current state (measured before this doc)

- `MapScene.drawWall` (`web/src/game/MapScene.ts`) draws the plane with `wallN/W{1,2,4}` masks or
  the textured `wall:<key>:<edge>:<cap|fill>:<variant>` cells, then, only when the wall reaches its
  full `MAX_WALL_H`, one `lineN/W` mask (`edgeLineMask`) tinted `color × 1.12`. Stubs and walls cut
  by the cutoff get no top at all.
- The visible face of a north wall faces +y (into its own tile); a west wall's faces +x. The
  camera never sees faces pointing −x or −y.
- `changedChunkKeys` (`web/src/game/dirty.ts`) redraws a changed chunk and its four orthogonal
  neighbours only.
- No visual spec frames a wall: `spawn.spec.ts` shows the spawn building's roof.

## 1. Decisions

| Topic | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Thickness | `WALL_T` = 1/8 m, one constant. On screen: 4 px along the edge's depth axis, so the top strip is 4 px tall at x1, 8 px at x2, 16 px at x4 | "A bit thicker": four times today's top. 1/4 m (8/16/32 px) is the alternative if 1/8 still reads thin; only the constant and the mask tests change |
| Direction | The body grows **outward**, behind the visible face: a north wall into −y, a west wall into −x | The drawn face, cutaway, occlusion and Niko's 0.3 m clearance all stay on the edge. Growing inward would push the face into the room and over Niko |
| Top strip | Every drawn wall gets a strip at its drawn top: full height, stub top or cutoff. Flat `color × 1.12` | A cut wall showing its section is what makes it read as solid; 1.12 is today's `line` tint |
| `line` mask | Removed with `edgeLineMask`; the strip replaces it | Dead once the strip exists |
| End face | Drawn at a run's visible end: the east end of a north wall (faces +x, light 0.66) and the south end of a west wall (faces +y, light 0.82), over the exposed height range [Sec. 2] | Doorways and wall ends are where thickness shows most |
| Corner post | A `WALL_T × WALL_T` top where a tile's own north and west walls meet, only when both have the same drawn top | That back corner is the only gap two outward strips leave [Sec. 2] |
| Texture | Strip, end face and post are flat tints even on textured (brick) walls | 4 px of brick is noise; the plane keeps its texture |
| Windows | End faces skip the glass band `base+2..base+4`; the strip sits on the top as usual | Matches the plane's opening |

## 2. Geometry

World coordinates, tile (x, y), `t = WALL_T`. Screen: +x is (+32, +16) px, +y is (−32, +16) px,
so `t` along −y is (+4, −2) and along −x is (−4, −2).

- **North strip** at drawn top: `[x, x+1] × [y−t, y]`. **West strip**: `[x−t, x] × [y, y+1]`.
- **Post**: `[x−t, x] × [y−t, y]`. Checking the four ways two strips can meet at a vertex, only
  `wall_n(x,y)` + `wall_w(x,y)` leaves this square empty; every other pair already covers it.
- **North end face** on plane `x+1`, spanning `y−t..y`. Hidden when `wall_n(x+1, y)` continues
  the run or `wall_w(x+1, y−1)` stands on that plane (its face already covers the sliver; a flat
  sliver over brick would show).
- **West end face** on plane `y+1`, spanning `x−t..x`. Hidden when `wall_w(x, y+1)` continues or
  `wall_n(x−1, y+1)` covers it.
- **Exposed range** of an end face: `[max(base, coverTop), top]`, where `top` is this wall's drawn
  top and `coverTop` is the highest drawn top of the two walls above (their `base` if absent or a
  doorway). This handles a full wall next to a cut stub: the part above the stub shows.
- "Shown" means non-zero material without the matching `EDGE_*_DOORWAY` flag, on the same level
  `z`, read through `ChunkStore.levelCell`, which crosses chunk borders.
- **Drawn top** of any wall: the value `drawWall` computes today (`MAX_WALL_H` cap, the tile's
  `cutoffAt`, `isFrontWall` stub). Factor it into one `drawnWallTop(edge, x, y, z)` used by both
  the wall and its neighbours, so a neighbour's top is never recomputed differently.

## 3. Client

**web.game** (`tileMasks.ts`, new `wallJoints.ts`, `MapScene.ts`, `dirty.ts`)
- `tileMasks.ts`: export `WALL_T_PX = 4`. Add, in the same tile-local frame as `wallMask`:
  - `wallTopMask(edge)`: the strip parallelogram; its front row is the old `topRow` line.
    Rasterize by pixel centre against the continuous parallelogram so the pixel count is exact.
  - `wallEndMask(face: "e" | "s", units: 1 | 2 | 4)`: the vertical sliver, `WALL_T_PX` wide,
    `16 × units` tall, anchored at the right vertex (`"e"`) or left vertex (`"s"`) like `wallMask`.
  - `wallPostMask()`: the small rhombus above the diamond's top vertex.
  - Remove `edgeLineMask`.
- `wallJoints.ts` (pure, no Phaser): `wallJoints(edge, x, y, z, lookup)` returns
  `{ end: { from: number; to: number } | null; post: boolean }` from a `lookup` giving each
  neighbour's shown flag, base and drawn top. `MapScene` passes a lookup built on `levelCell` and
  `drawnWallTop`.
- `MapScene.createTerrainAtlas`: register `wallTop{N,W}`, `wallEnd{E,S}{1,2,4}` and `wallPost`;
  drop `line{N,W}`.
- `MapScene.drawWall`: after the plane (and window band), draw the end face in 4/2/1 runs like
  `drawWallRun`, then the strip at `top`, then, from the north-wall call, the post. All in the
  owning tile's `row`, so the order is plane → end → strip → post.
- `chunkBounds` needs no change: its bbox is already padded by a full tile.
- `dirty.ts` `changedChunkKeys`: also mark the diagonal chunks `(cx−1, cy+1)` and `(cx+1, cy−1)`.
  A tile at a chunk corner reads `(x+1, y−1)` and `(x−1, y+1)` for its end faces; without this,
  breaking a wall in a diagonal chunk leaves a stale end face.

No `server.*`, `web.world`, `web.net`, `web.ui` or schema change.

## 4. What must not break

- The wall plane's pixels, textured cells and window band are identical to today.
- Picking, occlusion (`web/src/world/occlusion.ts`), the structure cutaway and physics still treat
  the wall as the edge: right-click on the tile in front of a wall picks that tile, not the strip.
- A front wall near Niko is still cut to a one-unit stub, now with a strip on the cut.
- Doorways stay open; breaking a wall redraws its neighbours' end faces, across chunk borders too.
- The terrain atlas stays within its bands: the new masks are small and live in the mask row.
- Spawn baselines do not change (no wall in frame); if they do, find out why before re-shooting.

## 5. Acceptance

### Automated

1. `web/tests/tile-masks.test.ts`: `wallTopMask("n"|"w")` has 128 px (area `32 × 16 ⋅ t`), every
   full column is `WALL_T_PX` px tall and its lowest pixel sits on `topRow(X)`; the two strips are
   mirror images; `wallEndMask(face, u)` has `64 × u` px; `wallPostMask()` has 16 px and touches
   both strips without overlapping the diamond; `edgeLineMask` is gone.
2. `web/tests/wall-thickness.test.ts` (`wallJoints`): a lone north wall shows its full end face;
   a continued run shows none; a covering perpendicular wall hides it; a doorway neighbour shows
   it; a stub neighbour exposes `[stubTop, top]`; a window wall's end face skips `base+2..base+4`;
   the post appears only for a tile's own north + west pair with equal tops.
3. `web/tests/dirty.test.ts`: `changedChunkKeys(chunks, "0,0")` also includes the two diagonal
   neighbours when they exist.
4. `npm run check` and `npm run check:visual` (spawn baselines unchanged).

### Manual

5. Manual (GUI, x1/x2/x4): room walls read as solid with a visible top; door openings and wall
   ends show their thickness; corners have no notch or overhang; brick walls keep their courses.
   Reason: no visual spec frames a wall, and adding one needs a camera path to a building interior
   that the spawn spec does not have.
6. Manual: walk Niko next to a front wall; the stub shows its cut strip, and walls behind him are
   unchanged. Reason: cutaway depends on live movement.

## 9. Out of scope

- Physical thickness (collision, picking or occlusion by the wall body).
- Window sills and door jambs drawn as recesses inside the opening.
- The post when the north and west walls have different drawn tops (only while one is cut): a
  4 px notch is accepted there.
- A visual spec that frames a building interior.

---

## TL;DR

Walls get a visible 1/8 m body, render-only and growing outward so the face stays on its edge: a
flat top strip on every drawn top (full, stub or cut), end faces where a run stops or meets a
doorway, and a small post at a tile's north-west corner. `web.game` only; the 1 px `line` goes,
diagonal chunks join the redraw set, and the new geometry is covered by mask and joint tests plus
a manual GUI pass.
