# Dev-024: Baked ambient occlusion

[Feature] [Rendering] [Performance]

Terraces read flat. Height shading (`shadeColor`) tints a whole top by its `h`, and faces get a
fixed side light (S 0.82, E 0.66), but nothing darkens the **creases**: the foot of a cliff, the
floor along a wall, a room's inner corners, the ground under a chest. Those contact darks are what
the eye uses to judge depth. This doc adds ambient occlusion **baked per tile at chunk draw time**,
drawn with the same `Blitter` batches the terrain already uses: no shader, no extra pass, zero
per-frame cost.

Reference: `docs/utils/VISION.md` [Sec. 5] (terraces, height shading); `docs/utils/PITFALLS.md`
[Sec. 5] (renderer); `docs/done/Dev-008.md` (isometric renderer); `docs/done/Fix05.md` (batches);
`CONTEXT.md`.

## 0. Findings (measured 25/09/2026)

| # | Where | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | `chunkRenderer.ts` `drawChunk` | Tops, faces, walls and objects are bobs in per-chunk `Blitter` batches (`base` plus one per row); each batch is one draw call, and a bob is one quad |
| F2 | `terrainAtlas.ts` | Masks are white RGB with an alpha shape, tinted per bob (`bob.setTint`, `bob.alpha`). A black-tinted alpha gradient darkens what is under it |
| F3 | `iso.ts` | Tile corners on screen: `(x, y)` top, `(x+1, y)` right, `(x+1, y+1)` bottom, `(x, y+1)` left. So the north edge is upper-right, west upper-left, south lower-left, east lower-right |
| F4 | `ChunkStore.isSolid(x, y, h)` | Answers "is half-metre cell `h` of this column solid" for ground, slabs and solid objects; `rules.ts` `wallOnEdge` answers the same for edges |
| F5 | `dirty.ts` `changedChunkKeys` | Redraws the 4 orthogonal and the NE/SW diagonal neighbours of a changed chunk, not NW/SE. Faces only look east and south, so that was enough until now |
| F6 | Phaser 3 | No depth buffer exists for 2D sprites. Screen-space AO would need a separate depth/normal render target and a full-screen shader every frame |

## 1. Decisions

| Topic | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Technique | Voxel-style corner/edge AO, computed on the CPU per drawn surface and baked into the chunk batches | The world is a half-metre grid: occluders are known exactly (F4). Cost is paid once per chunk redraw, never per frame (F1) |
| Not SSAO | Rejected | F6: a full-screen pass every frame, blurry halos on pixel art, and hidden storeys (cutaway) would still darken what is shown |
| Occluder | For a surface at `h`, a neighbour occludes when its cell `h+1` is solid (F4) **as drawn**: cells above that neighbour's `cutoffAt` do not count. A full (non-doorway) wall on the shared edge whose base is at `h` also occludes. Strength: `h+2` also solid → strong, otherwise soft | AO must follow the drawn geometry, or a cut building would leave dark ghosts on the floor |
| Bits | 4 edge bits (N, W, S, E) and 4 corner bits (NW, NE, SW, SE). A corner bit is set only when its diagonal occludes and neither adjacent edge does | Classic voxel rule: an edge gradient already covers its corners, and stacking them double-darkens |
| Frames | 8 top frames (`ao:top:n` … `ao:top:se`), 2 face frames (`ao:face:s`, `ao:face:e`), 2 wall frames (`ao:wall:n`, `ao:wall:w`), 1 contact blob (`ao:blob`). All pure alpha masks generated in `tileMasks.ts`, like the existing ones | 13 small frames fit in the existing mask slot row; no new art, no BitCanvas change |
| Gradient | Tile-local `(u, v)` from the diamond pixel; edge alpha `255·(1 − d/0.35)²` for distance `d` to the edge, corner alpha is the product of its two edge terms. Face and wall frames fade over the bottom unit (16 px) | Quadratic falloff reads as soft light; clipped to the diamond, so it never bleeds onto a neighbour |
| Intensity | One bob per set bit, tint `0x000000`, alpha from a table: soft 0.16, strong 0.30 (tops); faces and walls 0.22; blob 0.28. Tunable constants in one place | Alpha per bob gives two strengths from one frame set (F2) |
| Faces | The bottom unit of an S/E face run gets `ao:face:*` where the face meets a drawn lower surface. Void-cut faces (`fillOnly`) and faces at the world edge get none | Darkens the other side of the same crease the lower top already shows |
| Walls | The bottom unit of a drawn wall (stubs included) gets `ao:wall:*`, windows skip the glass band | Floor-to-wall joints are the strongest depth cue indoors |
| Objects | A solid object with a sprite or prism gets `ao:blob` at its base, drawn right before it in the same batch. Objects are **not** edge occluders for neighbour tops | Sprites do not fill the tile; a full-edge dark next to a thin table looks wrong |
| Actors | Out of scope [Sec. 2] | Actor `Graphics` interact with the silhouette and Fix14/Fix16 occlusion; a separate small doc |
| Order | Every AO bob goes into the same `Blitter` and row as the surface it darkens, right after it (`blitterFor` with the same `h`) | PITFALLS [Sec. 5]: batches keep display order at equal depth, so a later bob in the same batch lands on top of exactly its surface and under the next row |
| Toggle | `ao` on by default; the `DEBUG` view gets an `AO` checkbox that sets it and redraws every chunk (`materialChunkKeys`) | Lets the user compare, and bisect a visual bug in one click |

## 2. Out of scope

- Actor contact shadows (Niko, Extras) and the physics marker: next doc if the result is liked.
- Directional sun shadows (cast along a light vector): a different feature, much wider reach.
- Dev-021's midline and diagonal walls: when Dev-021 lands, its lines become occluders too; that
  doc gains one line in its web.game section. Dev-024 does not wait for it.
- Any server change. AO is visual only, like texture mappings (PITFALLS [Sec. 5]).

## 3. What changes

### 3.1 web.game: new `ambientOcclusion.ts`

- Pure, node-testable, no Phaser import. `AoView = { isSolid, wallOnEdge, cutoffAt }` so tests
  pass a fake store.
- `topBits(view, x, y, h): { edges: AoEdge[]; corners: AoCorner[] }` where each entry carries
  `strength: "soft" | "strong"`. Implements [Sec. 1, Occluder] and [Sec. 1, Bits].
- `faceBase(view, side, x, y, h0): boolean` and `wallBase(...)`: whether the bottom unit gets AO.
- `AO_ALPHA` table [Sec. 1, Intensity].
- **Cost control.** `drawChunk` builds one `AoGrid` per call: a flat `Int16Array` of
  `(size + 2)²` entries per distinct surface height band in the chunk, holding each column's
  solid-run length above that band (0, 1 or 2), including the 1-tile border from the neighbour
  chunks. Lookups are array reads, not `ChunkStore` scans. Walls are read once per edge from the
  level arrays already in hand.

### 3.2 web.game: `tileMasks.ts`, `terrainAtlas.ts`

- `aoTopMask(bit)`, `aoFaceMask(side)`, `aoWallMask(edge)`, `aoBlobMask()` return `TileMask`
  with graded alpha (today every mask pixel is 0 or 255). Face and wall frames reuse
  `faceMask(side, 1)` / `wallMask(edge, 1)` as the shape and grade it by row.
- `buildTerrainAtlas` pushes the 13 frames into `masks`. The slot row grows by 13 × 64 px;
  check it stays under the 4096 px guard with today's sheets (6 × 4 sprite slots + 27 masks + 13).

### 3.3 web.game: `chunkRenderer.ts`, `wallPainter.ts`

- `drawSurface`: after the top bob, draw one AO bob per bit.
- `drawVertical`: on the unit that starts at `h0`, one `ao:face:*` bob after the side unit.
- `drawTileObjects`: `ao:blob` before each solid object.
- `WallPainter.drawWall`: `ao:wall:*` after the bottom unit. `WallPaint` gains `aoEnabled()`.
- `ChunkRendererView` gains `readonly ao: boolean`; everything above is skipped when it is false.

### 3.4 web.game: `dirty.ts`, `MapScene.ts`

- `changedChunkKeys` also returns the NW and SE diagonals (F5): a corner bit reads them.
- `MapScene` exposes `ao` through the view and redraws all chunks when it changes.

### 3.5 web.ui

- `DebugViews.tsx`: `AO` checkbox bound to the scene flag. No new API, no schema change.

### 3.6 Docs

- `docs/utils/VISION.md` [Sec. 5], Surface: "Height shading stays" becomes "Height shading and
  baked ambient occlusion at creases stay". One sentence, approved with this doc.
- `docs/utils/PITFALLS.md` [Sec. 5]: "AO follows the drawn geometry, not the stored one" and
  "AO bobs share their surface's batch and row".
- `CONTEXT.md`: `ambientOcclusion.ts` in the web.game row.

## 4. What must not break

- **Picking, movement, menus.** AO is paint only. `pick.ts`, `rules.ts`, `occlusion.ts` and
  `cutaway.ts` are not touched.
- **Cutaway.** No AO from a cut storey; a front-wall stub still casts its own wall AO only.
- **Draw order.** No AO bob ever covers a wall, object or face of a later row.
- **Frame time.** No per-frame work is added: AO runs only inside `drawChunk`.
- **Redraw budget.** `drawChunk` time grows by at most 25 % on the seed-7 spawn chunk
  (measured in the node benchmark [Sec. 5] 6).
- **Existing tests** pass unchanged, except the visual baselines, which are re-shot on purpose.

## 5. Acceptance

1. `web/tests/ambient-occlusion.test.ts`: a flat tile has no bits; a 0.5 m step north gives
   soft `n`; a 1 m step gives strong `n`; a higher diagonal alone gives its corner; a corner is
   dropped when an adjacent edge is set; a wall on the west edge gives `w`; a doorway gives none.
2. Same file: a neighbour cut by `cutoffAt` below `h+1` casts nothing; a void-cut face gets no
   face AO.
3. `web/tests/tile-masks.test.ts`: every AO mask stays inside its base shape (diamond, face,
   wall); alpha is 0 at distance ≥ 0.35 and highest on its edge or corner.
4. `web/tests/dirty.test.ts`: `changedChunkKeys` includes all 8 neighbours.
5. `web/tests/isometric-world.test.ts`: with `ao` off, `drawChunk` emits the same bobs as today;
   with it on, each AO bob sits in the same batch as, and right after, the surface it darkens.
6. `web/tests/ambient-occlusion.test.ts`: benchmark of `topBits` over a 32×32 building chunk,
   reported, plus the 25 % guard of [Sec. 4] on the `AoGrid` path.
7. `npm run check:visual`: re-shoot spawn x1/x2/x4 and building x1/x2 baselines; the user
   approves them (manual: taste).
8. Manual, in the GUI (frame-feel and taste cannot be tested headlessly): at the lab map's steps,
   structure and walls bays, cliff feet, wall bases and room corners read darker; toggling `AO`
   in `DEBUG` switches it live; walking through the building shows no dark ghost of a cut storey
   and no visible drop in smoothness at x1.

## TL;DR

Depth reads flat because creases are not darkened. Add voxel-style ambient occlusion computed per
tile from the half-metre grid when a chunk is drawn, and paint it as black alpha-gradient bobs in
the same `Blitter` batches: 8 edge/corner frames for tops, one for face bottoms, one for wall
bases and a contact blob under objects. It follows the drawn (cut) geometry, costs nothing per
frame, and has a `DEBUG` toggle. SSAO is rejected: no depth buffer, per-frame cost, halos on pixel
art. Actors' shadows and Dev-021's new wall lines come later.
