# Dev-021: Wall lines and wall heights

[Feature] [World] [Engine] [Rendering] [Migration]

Walls today live only on a tile's north and west edges and always rise a full storey. This doc
gives each tile six wall lines (two edges, two midlines, two diagonals) and three wall heights
(0.5 m, 1 m, full), so rooms can be sized in half metres, corners can be cut and low walls,
parapets and counters exist. It is the geometry the construction system builds on; how a wall
gets placed (the `build` op) is the next doc.

Reference: `docs/utils/VISION.md` [Sec. 5] (standing rule, walls on edges, physics);
`docs/Dev-020.md` (wall thickness, lands first); `docs/done/Dev-013.md` (wall integrity);
`docs/done/Dev-018.md` (Extras and A*); `CONTEXT.md`.

## 1. Decisions

| Topic | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Lines | Six per tile: `north`, `west` (edges, as today), `mid_ns` (x + 0.5, running north–south), `mid_we` (y + 0.5, running west–east), `diag_nwse` (corner (x, y) to (x+1, y+1)), `diag_nesw` ((x+1, y) to (x, y+1)). Every line is full length: 1 m, or 1.41 m for diagonals | The user's sketch: edges, the centre cross and both full diagonals. No half-length pieces |
| Heights | `full` (to the storey top, today's 6 cells), `1m` (2 cells), `semi` (0.5 m, 1 cell), measured from the wall's base as today | Half-metre cells are already the vertical unit |
| Walking | A wall blocks a body standing at `h` when it occupies any of cells `h+1..h+3`: `bottom < h + 4 and bottom + height > h + 1`. So a `semi` wall is stepped over like a 0.5 m step; `1m` and `full` block | It follows the existing standing rule. Real life: you step over a knee-high wall and vault a waist-high one |
| Full walls, behaviour change | The same test replaces `bottom + 6 > h`. A full wall whose top is only 0.5 m above a body's feet no longer blocks it | One rule for every height. It only matters when standing 2.5 m above a wall's base |
| Vault | `climb` also crosses a `1m` wall on a tile edge to a neighbour within a step of `h`. Interior lines are not vaulted in this doc | `climb` already covers 1–1.5 m rises; a 1 m wall is the same effort |
| Openings | Doorway and window flags on edges and midlines, only on `full` walls. Diagonals and low walls have none | Keeps diagonal collision a solid segment. Low walls have nothing to open |
| Diagonal on screen | `diag_nesw` projects horizontally and faces the camera. `diag_nwse` projects vertically and is seen end-on: with Dev-020's `WALL_T` it draws as an 8 px column at x1. That is accepted, not worked around | The camera is fixed (VISION [Sec. 3]); an end-on partition looks like that in reality |
| Diagonal texture | Diagonals are drawn with flat tinted masks, even for textured materials | Brick courses on a 45° face need new BitCanvas sheets, not needed to prove the geometry |
| Objects | A tile crossed by any interior line (midline or diagonal) cannot hold a solid object | Objects sit at the tile centre, which lies on every interior line |
| Dig and fill | Refused on a tile crossed by an interior line | The wall stands on that ground |
| Extras nav | A* over half-metre quadrants [Sec. 3] | Tile nodes cannot see a 1 m corridor made of two half tiles |

## 2. Data and storage

**server.world** (`chunk.py`, `grid.py`, `gen/`)
- `ChunkLevel` gains four uint16 material arrays, `wall_mid_ns`, `wall_mid_we`, `wall_diag_nwse`,
  `wall_diag_nesw`, and one uint16 array `wall_height`: 2 bits per line in the order `north`,
  `west`, `mid_ns`, `mid_we`, `diag_nwse`, `diag_nesw`. The codes are `0 = full`, `1 = semi` and
  `2 = 1m`; `3` is reserved and rejected. `0 = full` means every existing wall keeps its meaning
  with no data rewrite.
- `edge_flags` keeps its bits and uses the four free ones: `EDGE_MNS_DOORWAY = 16`,
  `EDGE_MNS_WINDOW = 32`, `EDGE_MWE_DOORWAY = 64`, `EDGE_MWE_WINDOW = 128`.
- One accessor, `WallLine`, replaces the scattered `wall_n`/`wall_w` reads: `line(x, y, z, name)`
  returns material, height in cells, base and flags. `grid._wall_on_edge` becomes
  `wall_blocks(line, x, y, h)` using the rule in [Sec. 1].
- `GenCanvas` `level.set_wall(x, y, line, material, height="full")` validates: code 3, openings on
  low walls or diagonals, and interior lines on a tile holding a solid object are errors.
- The lab `walls` bay adds one row per line (`mid_ns`, `mid_we`, `diag_nwse`, `diag_nesw`) at each
  height, a 1 m corridor between two midlines, a 0.5 m gap and a doorway on a midline.
  `GEN_VERSION` of the lab bumps. The seeded city generator is unchanged.

**server.db**: migration `0009_wall_lines` adds the five blob columns, filled with zeros for every
existing `chunk_level` row. `wall_integrity`'s check constraint widens from `north`/`west` to the
six line names (SQLite batch mode). A `0008` save upgrades with every wall, flag and damage row
intact.

## 3. Movement, nav and physics

**server.engine** `movement.py` and **web.world** `rules.ts`, kept in parity:
- Vertical and horizontal lines sit every 0.5 m. A sub-step whose centre crosses `x = k/2` checks
  the tile's `west` edge for even `k` and `mid_ns` for odd `k` (the same for `y`). A block clamps
  the centre to the line ± 0.3 m, as the edge clamp does today.
- Diagonals: a sub-step is blocked when the centre would change sides of a blocking diagonal
  inside that tile's square, or come closer than 0.3 m to it. The clamp along the moving axis sits
  at 0.3 m perpendicular distance (0.3·√2 along the axis). Movement stays axis-separated, so
  holding the key parallel to a diagonal still advances the body.
- `server/tests/world-parity.json` gains cases for every line, every height and the diagonal clamp.

**server.world** `nav.py`:
- Nodes are `(qx, qy, h)` with `qx = 2x + {0, 1}`; standing surfaces still come from the tile.
- A quadrant crossed by a diagonal is not a node: `diag_nwse` takes the NW and SE quadrants,
  `diag_nesw` takes the NE and SW. A quadrant with blocking lines on two opposite sides
  (a 0.5 m gap) is not a node either, because a 0.6 m body does not fit.
- An orthogonal move between quadrants is blocked by a blocking line on their shared half edge. A
  diagonal move needs both orthogonal moves, as today. Height steps between tiles are unchanged.
- Goals stay tiles; a goal is reached at any free quadrant of the tile. `MAX_EXPANSIONS` goes
  from 3000 to 12000. Extras steer to quadrant centres (`+0.25`/`+0.75`); `ARRIVE_METRES` (0.35)
  already covers the 0.05 m a wall clamp leaves.

**server.engine** physics and targets:
- New target `WallTarget {kind: "wall", x, y, z, line}` for the four interior lines. Edges keep
  `EdgeTarget`. The tile menu (`engine/menu.py` candidates) lists a tile's interior walls next to its
  edges; `hit` and `break` accept both.
- Wall integrity at full health is `resistance × height cells` (1, 2 or 6), whatever the line.
  Breaking a line clears its material, height code and flags in one commit.
- Trajectories: a moving body occupies `[h, h + 4)` and an object occupies `[h, h + kind.height)`.
  Any wall intersecting that range stops them, so a shove does trip over a `semi` wall that walking
  steps over. Entering a tile crossed by an interior line perpendicular to the travel direction
  meets that line at entry.

## 4. Client

**web.net**: regenerated types for the five new level arrays and `WallTarget`.

**web.world** (`ChunkStore.ts`, `rules.ts`, `occlusion.ts`, `cutaway.ts`)
- `ChunkStore` cells carry the six lines with height and flags through one `wallLine` accessor.
- Occlusion and the roofed rule count only `full` walls, on any line. Low walls never hide Niko
  and never count as walling.

**web.game** (`chunkRenderer.ts`, `wallPainter.ts`, `tileMasks.ts`, `wallJoints.ts`;
`MapScene.ts` orchestration)
- Midlines reuse the edge masks and Dev-020's strip and end faces, offset half a tile: `mid_ns`
  by (+16, +8) px, `mid_we` by (−16, +8) px at x1. `WallPainter` draws them, and
  `wallJoints` extends to the half-metre lattice.
- Diagonal masks: `diag_nesw` is a screen-aligned rectangle 64 px wide; `diag_nwse` is Dev-020's
  end-face column. `WallPainter` draws both, each with its top strip.
- Depth: midlines sort at their line (tile depth + 0.5), diagonals at the tile centre.
- The cap draws at the wall's real top, not only at `base + MAX_WALL_H`. Low walls are never cut
  to a stub. A front `full` midline or `diag_nesw` near Niko is stubbed like an edge; `diag_nwse`
  never is, because it hides nothing.

## 5. What must not break

- Existing saves: every edge wall, opening and damage row survives `0009` unchanged.
- Server and client agree on every move (`world-parity.json`).
- Open ground: routes and movement are unchanged where no interior line exists.
- The engine is the only writer; breaking and generation commit atomically, as today.
- Doorways on edges still let bodies through; windows still block.
- Dev-020's thickness still reads the same on edge walls.

## TL;DR

Each tile gets six full-length wall lines (edges, midlines, diagonals) and each wall one of three
heights (0.5 m, 1 m, full). The existing standing rule decides blocking, so a 0.5 m wall is stepped
over and a 1 m one is vaulted. Extras route over half-metre quadrants, physics scales integrity by
height, and migration `0009_wall_lines` keeps every save. Diagonals are drawn flat and accepted
end-on for the vertical one. Placing walls in play is the next doc.
