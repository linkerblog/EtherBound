# Dev-024: Baked ambient occlusion

[Feature] [Rendering] [Performance]

Terraces read flat. Height shading (`shadeColor`) tints a whole top by its `h`, and faces get a
fixed side light (S 0.82, E 0.66), but nothing darkens the **creases**: the foot of a cliff, the
floor along a wall, a room's inner corners, the ground under a chest. Those contact darks are what
the eye uses to judge depth. This doc adds ambient occlusion **baked per tile at chunk draw time**,
drawn with the same `Blitter` batches the terrain already uses: no shader, no extra pass, zero
per-frame cost.

Reference: `docs/utils/VISION.md` [Sec. 5] (terraces, height shading); `docs/utils/PITFALLS.md`
[Sec. 5] (renderer); `docs/done/Dev-008.md` (isometric renderer); `docs/done/Fix05.md` (batches);
`CONTEXT.md`.

## 0. Findings (measured 25/09/2026)

| # | Where | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | `chunkRenderer.ts` `drawChunk` | Tops, faces, walls and objects are bobs in per-chunk `Blitter` batches (`base` plus one per row); each batch is one draw call, and a bob is one quad |
| F2 | `terrainAtlas.ts` | Masks are white RGB with an alpha shape, tinted per bob (`bob.setTint`, `bob.alpha`). A black-tinted alpha gradient darkens what is under it |
| F3 | `iso.ts` | Tile corners on screen: `(x, y)` top, `(x+1, y)` right, `(x+1, y+1)` bottom, `(x, y+1)` left. So the north edge is upper-right, west upper-left, south lower-left, east lower-right |
| F4 | `ChunkStore.isSolid(x, y, h)` | Not usable as the AO occluder as-is. Ground cell `k` is solid when `k < ground_h`, but a slab is solid **at** its own `floor_h`, so the two disagree by one unit. An unloaded tile answers `true`. Solid objects count too |
| F5 | `dirty.ts` `changedChunkKeys` | Redraws the 4 orthogonal and the NE/SW diagonal neighbours of a changed chunk, not NW/SE. Faces only look east and south, so that was enough until now |
| F6 | Phaser 3 | No depth buffer exists for 2D sprites. Screen-space AO would need a separate depth/normal render target and a full-screen shader every frame |
| F7 | `rules.ts` `wallOnEdge` | Module-private. It answers `true` for an unloaded chunk, and its height test is the body window (`base < h + 4` and `base + 6 > h`), not "base at `h`" |
| F8 | `dirty.ts` `cutoffChunkKeys`, `tileChunkKeys` | Only redraw chunks **with levels**. A level-less chunk next to a building is not redrawn when the building's cutoff changes |
| F9 | `terrainAtlas.ts` slot row | Today it holds 6 sheets × 4 + 24 masks = 48 slots = 3072 px. Plus 13 AO frames it holds 61 slots = 3904 px. The side, wall and object bands have a 4096 px guard; the slot row has none |
| F10 | `drawTileObjects` | A whole sprite below the viewer goes to `base`, one above goes to a row batch, and a split sprite goes to both. There is no single "object batch" |
| F11 | `web/tests` | No test drives `drawChunk`: `chunkRenderer.ts` imports `phaser` as a value although it only uses its types, and `isometric-world.test.ts` covers pure world functions only |

## 1. Decisions

| Topic | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Technique | Voxel-style corner/edge AO, computed on the CPU per drawn surface and baked into the chunk batches | The world is a half-metre grid, so occluders are known exactly. Cost is paid once per chunk redraw, never per frame (F1) |
| Not SSAO | Rejected | F6: a full-screen pass every frame, blurry halos on pixel art, and hidden storeys (cutaway) would still darken what is shown |
| Occluder | Defined on surface heights, not on `isSolid` (F4). For a surface at `h`, a neighbour column **fills band `k`** (the half-metre up to `h + k`) when its drawn ground top (`solidTopH`) is `≥ h + k`, or it has a drawn slab with `floor_h = h + k`. "Drawn" means at or below that neighbour's `cutoffAt`, as `drawChunk` decides. Band 1 filled → soft; bands 1 and 2 filled → strong | AO must follow the drawn geometry, or a cut building leaves dark ghosts on the floor. Using `isSolid` would miss every 0.5 m ground step and double-count slabs |
| Wall occluder | A wall on the shared edge occludes a surface at `h` when its base equals `h`, it is not a doorway, and it is drawn (base at or below the owning tile's `cutoffAt`). Always strong. Read from the level arrays, not through `wallOnEdge` (F7) | Same rule as the renderer's own wall test, so AO appears exactly where a wall is painted |
| Unloaded neighbours | An unloaded or out-of-bounds neighbour **never** occludes. When its chunk arrives, `changedChunkKeys` redraws the border anyway | `isSolid` and `wallOnEdge` treat unloaded tiles as blocked (F4, F7). That would darken every streaming border and the world edge |
| Bits | 4 edge bits (N, W, S, E) and 4 corner bits (NW, NE, SW, SE). A corner bit is set only when its diagonal occludes and neither adjacent edge does | Classic voxel rule: an edge gradient already covers its corners, and stacking them double-darkens |
| Frames | 8 top frames (`ao:top:n` … `ao:top:se`), 2 face frames (`ao:face:s`, `ao:face:e`), 2 wall frames (`ao:wall:n`, `ao:wall:w`), 1 contact blob (`ao:blob`). All pure alpha masks generated in `tileMasks.ts`, like the existing ones | 13 small frames fit in the existing slot row (F9); no new art, no BitCanvas change |
| Atlas guard | `buildTerrainAtlas` gets the same 4096 px guard on the slot row as the other bands. If the AO frames do not fit, it warns and skips them, and the renderer skips any AO bob whose frame is missing | 3904 px leaves room for only 3 more slots (F9). A new sheet must not silently break the atlas |
| Gradient | Tile-local `(u, v)` from the diamond pixel; edge alpha `255·(1 − d/0.35)²` for distance `d` to the edge, corner alpha is the product of its two edge terms. Face and wall frames fade over the bottom unit (16 px) | Quadratic falloff reads as soft light; clipped to the diamond, so it never bleeds onto a neighbour |
| Intensity | One bob per set bit, tint `0x000000`, alpha from a table: soft 0.16, strong 0.30 (tops); faces and walls 0.22; blob 0.28. Tunable constants in one place | Alpha per bob gives two strengths from one frame set (F2) |
| Faces | The bottom unit of an S/E face run gets `ao:face:*` where the face meets a drawn lower surface. Void-cut faces (`fillOnly`) and faces at the world edge get none | Darkens the other side of the same crease the lower top already shows |
| Walls | The bottom unit of a drawn wall (stubs included) gets `ao:wall:*`, windows skip the glass band | Floor-to-wall joints are the strongest depth cue indoors |
| Objects | A solid object with a sprite or prism, whose resting surface is drawn (`restingSurfaceDrawn`), gets `ao:blob`. The blob goes into the batch of its **resting surface** (`blitterFor(row, object.h)`), before the first sprite or prism piece of that object. Objects are **not** occluders for neighbour tops | F10: there is no single object batch. The blob darkens the floor, so it belongs with the floor. Sprites do not fill the tile; a full-edge dark next to a thin table looks wrong |
| Actors | Out of scope [Sec. 2] | Actor `Graphics` interact with the silhouette and Fix14/Fix16 occlusion; a separate small doc |
| Order | Every AO bob goes into the same `Blitter` and row as the surface it darkens, right after it (`blitterFor` with the same `h`) | PITFALLS [Sec. 5]: batches keep display order at equal depth, so a later bob in the same batch lands on top of exactly its surface and under the next row |
| Invalidation | With `ao` on, `cutoffChunkKeys` also returns level-less chunks bordering a chunk with levels. `tileChunkKeys` also returns level-less chunks that border a chunk with levels and touch its tile window grown by one tile | F8: a border tile's AO reads the building's cutoff. Limiting it to bordering chunks keeps the redraw count per step low |
| Testability | `chunkRenderer.ts` imports `phaser` type-only, so a node test can drive `drawChunk` with a fake scene whose blitters record their bobs | F11: without it neither the bob-order check nor the redraw budget can be tested |
| Toggle | `ao` on by default; the `DEBUG` view gets an `AO` checkbox that sets it and redraws every chunk (`materialChunkKeys`) | Lets the user compare, and bisect a visual bug in one click |

## 2. Out of scope

- Actor contact shadows (Niko, Extras) and the physics marker: next doc if the result is liked.
- Directional sun shadows (cast along a light vector): a different feature, much wider reach.
- Dev-021's midline and diagonal walls: when Dev-021 lands, its lines become occluders too; that
  doc gains one line in its web.game section. Dev-024 does not wait for it.
- Any server change. AO is visual only, like texture mappings (PITFALLS [Sec. 5]).
- Changing `ChunkStore.isSolid` or `wallOnEdge`. Their slab/ground asymmetry and their answer for
  unloaded tiles serve movement and picking; AO reads the chunk arrays itself.

## 3. What changes

### 3.1 web.game: new `ambientOcclusion.ts`

- Pure, node-testable, no Phaser import. `AoView = { chunk(cx, cy), solidTopH, isVoid, cutoffAt }`
  so tests pass a fake store. It does **not** call `isSolid` or `wallOnEdge` (F4, F7).
- `topBits(grid, x, y, h): { edges: AoEdge[]; corners: AoCorner[] }` where each entry carries
  `strength: "soft" | "strong"`. Implements [Sec. 1, Occluder], [Sec. 1, Wall occluder],
  [Sec. 1, Unloaded neighbours] and [Sec. 1, Bits].
- `faceBase(grid, side, x, y, h0): boolean` and `wallBase(...)`: whether the bottom unit gets AO.
- `AO_ALPHA` table [Sec. 1, Intensity].
- **Cost control.** `drawChunk` builds one `AoGrid` per call from the chunk and its 8 neighbours:
  for every column of the `(size + 2)²` window (the chunk plus a 1-tile border), the drawn ground
  top, the drawn slab heights, the drawn walls per edge with their bases, and an "unloaded" flag.
  All from the chunk arrays and `cutoffAt`, read once per column. `topBits` then does array
  reads only, not `ChunkStore` scans.

### 3.2 web.game: `tileMasks.ts`, `terrainAtlas.ts`

- `aoTopMask(bit)`, `aoFaceMask(side)`, `aoWallMask(edge)`, `aoBlobMask()` return `TileMask`
  with graded alpha (today every mask pixel is 0 or 255). Face and wall frames reuse
  `faceMask(side, 1)` / `wallMask(edge, 1)` as the shape and grade it by row.
- `buildTerrainAtlas` pushes the 13 frames into `masks`, 48 → 61 slots, 3072 → 3904 px (F9).
- New slot-row guard [Sec. 1, Atlas guard]: warn and skip the AO frames if the row would pass
  4096 px.

### 3.3 web.game: `chunkRenderer.ts`, `wallPainter.ts`

- `import type Phaser from "phaser"` [Sec. 1, Testability].
- `drawSurface`: after the top bob, draw one AO bob per bit.
- `drawVertical`: on the unit that starts at `h0`, one `ao:face:*` bob after the side unit.
- `drawTileObjects`: `ao:blob` in the resting surface's batch, before the object's first piece
  [Sec. 1, Objects].
- `WallPainter.drawWall`: `ao:wall:*` after the bottom unit. `WallPaint` gains `aoEnabled()`.
- `ChunkRendererView` gains `readonly ao: boolean`; everything above is skipped when it is false,
  and any single AO bob is skipped when its frame is not in the atlas.

### 3.4 web.game: `dirty.ts`, `MapScene.ts`

- `changedChunkKeys` also returns the NW and SE diagonals (F5): a corner bit reads them.
- `cutoffChunkKeys` and `tileChunkKeys` take an `ao` flag and add the level-less bordering chunks
  [Sec. 1, Invalidation].
- `MapScene` exposes `ao` through the view, passes it to the dirty helpers and redraws all chunks
  when it changes.

### 3.5 web.ui

- `DebugViews.tsx`: `AO` checkbox bound to the scene flag. No new API, no schema change.

### 3.6 Docs

- `docs/utils/VISION.md` [Sec. 5], Surface: "Height shading stays" becomes "Height shading and
  baked ambient occlusion at creases stay". One sentence, approved with this doc.
- `docs/utils/PITFALLS.md` [Sec. 5]:
  - "AO follows the drawn geometry, not the stored one".
  - "AO bobs share their surface's batch and row".
  - "AO does not use `isSolid`: ground and slabs disagree by one unit, and unloaded means blocked".
- `CONTEXT.md`: `ambientOcclusion.ts` in the web.game row.

## 4. What must not break

- **Picking, movement, menus.** AO is paint only. `pick.ts`, `rules.ts`, `occlusion.ts`,
  `cutaway.ts` and `ChunkStore.isSolid` are not touched.
- **Cutaway.** No AO from a cut storey, including across a chunk border; a front-wall stub still
  casts its own wall AO only.
- **Streaming.** No dark rim along the edge of the loaded area or the world bounds.
- **Draw order.** No AO bob ever covers a wall, object or face of a later row.
- **Frame time.** No per-frame work is added: AO runs only inside `drawChunk`.
- **Redraw budget.** `drawChunk` time grows by at most 25 % on the seed-7 spawn chunk, measured
  on the fake-scene harness [Sec. 5] 6. The extra chunks redrawn per step inside a building are
  counted in [Sec. 5] 4.
- **Existing tests** pass unchanged, except the visual baselines, which are re-shot on purpose.

## 5. Acceptance

1. `web/tests/ambient-occlusion.test.ts`:
   - A flat tile has no bits.
   - A 0.5 m **ground** step north gives soft `n`; a 1 m step gives strong `n`.
   - A neighbour **slab** at `h + 1` gives soft; a slab at `h` gives none.
   - A higher diagonal alone gives its corner; a corner is dropped when an adjacent edge is set.
   - A wall on the west edge gives `w`; a doorway gives none.
   - A solid object on the neighbour tile gives none.
2. Same file:
   - A neighbour cut by `cutoffAt` below its top casts nothing.
   - A void-cut face gets no face AO.
   - A neighbour in an unloaded chunk casts nothing.
3. `web/tests/tile-masks.test.ts`: every AO mask stays inside its base shape (diamond, face,
   wall); alpha is 0 at distance ≥ 0.35 and highest on its edge or corner. A fake atlas build with
   one sheet too many skips the AO frames with a warning instead of passing 4096 px.
4. `web/tests/dirty.test.ts`:
   - `changedChunkKeys` includes all 8 neighbours.
   - With `ao` on, `cutoffChunkKeys` and `tileChunkKeys` add a level-less chunk bordering a
     building, and leave out one that does not border it.
5. `web/tests/chunk-renderer.test.ts` (new, fake scene [Sec. 1, Testability]):
   - With `ao` off, `drawChunk` emits the same bobs as before.
   - With it on, each AO bob sits in the same batch as, and right after, the surface it darkens.
   - An object blob sits in its resting surface's batch before the object's first piece.
6. Same file: benchmark of `drawChunk` on the seed-7 spawn chunk and a 32×32 building chunk, `ao`
   off versus on, reported, with the 25 % guard of [Sec. 4].
7. `npm run check:visual`: re-shoot spawn x1/x2/x4 and building x1/x2 baselines; the user
   approves them (manual: taste).
8. Manual, in the GUI (frame-feel and taste cannot be tested headlessly):
   - At the lab map's steps, structure and walls bays, cliff feet, wall bases and room corners
     read darker.
   - Toggling `AO` in `DEBUG` switches it live.
   - Walking through the building shows no dark ghost of a cut storey, also at a chunk border,
     and no visible drop in smoothness at x1.
   - Streaming in new chunks leaves no dark rim.

## TL;DR

Depth reads flat because creases are not darkened. Add voxel-style ambient occlusion computed per
tile from the half-metre grid when a chunk is drawn, and paint it as black alpha-gradient bobs in
the same `Blitter` batches: 8 edge/corner frames for tops, one for face bottoms, one for wall
bases and a contact blob under objects. Occluders are read from the chunk arrays, not
`isSolid`. `isSolid` would miss 0.5 m ground steps, count objects and darken every unloaded
border. AO follows the drawn (cut) geometry, including across chunk borders, costs nothing per
frame, has a `DEBUG` toggle and a fake-scene test harness. SSAO is rejected: no depth buffer,
per-frame cost, halos on pixel art. Actors' shadows and Dev-021's new wall lines come later.
