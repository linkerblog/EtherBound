# Dev-018: Basic Extras (bodies, goals and a routine brain)

[Feature] [NPC] [Engine] [Renderer]

The world has one actor: Niko. This doc adds the first Extras (VISION [Sec. 8], NPC tiers): a few
seeded people who exist as ordinary actors, walk and wait through the same action API as Niko,
keep a persistent goal, and are drawn, inspected, pushed and hit like any other body. Their brain
is deliberately small: a deterministic routine that wanders near home and waits. It is the
skeleton that needs, traits and utility (`Dev-019`) plug into; nothing here is a scripted behaviour
that Dev-019 would have to remove.

Reference: `docs/utils/VISION.md` [Sec. 4] (architecture), [Sec. 8] (NPC tiers, three brains,
plan loop), [Sec. 11] (determinism); `CONTEXT.md` (Action API, Activities, Event bus, WebSocket);
`docs/PENDING.md` (Event log retention).

## 0. Repo state (measured 23/09/2026, v1.1.0)

- `actor` rows: only `niko` (`kind = "player"`), created by `WorldEngine._ensure_actor`
  (`engine/world.py:450`). `new_game` deletes every actor and recreates Niko.
- `WorldEngine.submit(actor_id, action, delta_seconds)` already accepts any actor id. Walking is
  `move {dx, dy}` at 4 m/s real time × `delta_seconds` (`engine/ops/move.py`), independent of the
  clock speed.
- `advance_time` completes activities of **every** actor in id order, then emits `clock.ticked`;
  it drains the bus outside the engine lock, so a subscriber may call `submit`.
- Physics ops already target adjacent actors (`engine/ops/physics.py`); their menu `subject` shows
  `actor.kind`. `inspect` targets tiles and objects only. Menu candidates include adjacent actors
  only, not actors on the clicked tile.
- `find_path(grid, start, goal, max_expansions)` (`world/nav.py:39`) is A* over standing spots.
- `tick`/`snapshot` carry every actor. The client draws only Niko (`MapScene.ts:151`) and picks the
  player with `state.actors.player ?? state.actors.niko ?? Object.values(state.actors)[0]`
  (`MapScene.ts:228`): the last fallback would pick an Extra.
- `new_game` always starts unpaused, and `check:visual` shoots the seed-7 spawn a moment later.

## 1. Decisions

| Topic | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | Bodies, persistence, goals, a routine brain, rendering and inspection. No needs, traits, relationships, talk, knowledge or schedules | Each of those is a primitive of its own; this doc gives them an actor to live in |
| Actor kind | `kind = "extra"`, ids `extra-001`…, a `name`, 80 kg | Same table, same API, same physics as Niko [VISION Sec. 2, pillar 4] |
| Population | `world/population.py`: `populate(grid, registry, spawn, seed) -> tuple[GeneratedActor, ...]`, `EXTRA_COUNT = 6`, independent of the generator | Generators build space; who lives there is a separate pass the city generator will feed later. Keeps `test` world bytes and the lab version unchanged |
| Placement | Seeded stream `population`: walkable standing ground tiles within 15 m of the spawn, reachable from it by `find_path`, not the spawn tile, at least 2 m from each other. Each Extra's `anchor` (home spot) is its start tile | Near Niko so they are seen; reachable so the brain never starts stuck |
| Names | `world/names.toml` (32 given, 32 family names), drawn from the same stream | Data, not code; `inspect` needs something to say |
| Save format | Migration `0008_extra`: `actor.name` (String(64), nullable) and `actor.mind` (JSON, nullable) | A goal must survive a restart [VISION Sec. 8: reactions become persistent goals] |
| Mind shape | `mind = {anchor: {x, y, h}, goal: {kind: "wander", x, y, h} \| null}`, Pydantic `Mind`/`Goal` in `engine/actions.py` | One goal kind today; Dev-019 adds kinds, not a second store |
| Goal writes | `WorldEngine.set_goal(actor_id, goal, reason)` validates (actor exists and is not the player, goal tile has a walkable standing surface at `h`) and commits `actor.goal_set` in the same transaction. The brain never touches the database | The engine stays the only writer; a goal is an intention, not an act, so it is not an op. Logged, so "why did she walk there" is answerable [VISION Sec. 11] |
| Brain location | New module `server.minds`, `minds/extras.py`: `ExtrasBrain`, a `clock.ticked` subscriber registered **before** `websocket.clock_ticked` | A decision source like the player [VISION Sec. 4]; the order makes each tick broadcast carry that tick's steps |
| Acting | Only `engine.submit(extra_id, action, delta_seconds)` with `move` and `wait`. A rejected action is data for the brain, never retried the same tick | One door for everyone |
| Walking speed | Per tick an Extra walks the time Niko would in that real interval: `tick_seconds = time_scale / world.speed`, budget `4 m/s × tick_seconds`. The brain splits the budget along the path into at most 6 `move` submits, each with `delta_seconds = min(remaining, segment_length / 4)` | Same speed and same time distortion as Niko [VISION Sec. 3, Distance]; deterministic from the logged speed |
| Path | `find_path` from the Extra's spot to the goal, `max_expansions = 3000`; consecutive steps in one direction merge into a segment. Cached in memory, recomputed when the Extra's tile is off the cached path (e.g. after a shove) | Paths are derived from state, so a restart recomputes the same one |
| Routine | Idle Extra with no goal: with `rng = stream(f"extras:{id}:{game_minute}")`, 60 % pick a wander goal (up to 5 tries: a tile within 12 m of `anchor`, standable and reachable), else `submit(wait)` (15 min). On arrival (within 0.35 m of the goal tile centre): `set_goal(None, "arrived")` then `wait`. An Extra with a running activity is skipped | The idle layer of Dev-019's utility; per-actor-minute streams make decisions independent of processing order and of restarts |
| Stuck | A segment that advances less than 0.05 m recomputes the path once; if A* fails or the retry also stalls, `set_goal(None, "stuck")` | Anti-loop from VISION [Sec. 8] without a persisted counter |
| Collisions | Extras and Niko pass through each other. Recorded in `PENDING.md` | Body-to-body blocking changes the standing rule on both sides; its own doc |
| Menus | `menu()` adds actors standing on the clicked tile at that `z` as candidates. `inspect` accepts `actor {id}` within 30 m: `"<name>. <state>."` where state is `Walking`, `Waiting` or `Standing`. Physics `subject` shows the name (Niko's is `Niko`) | Generated menus; Extras get entries through the handlers, no special case |
| Wire | `ActorSnapshot.name: str \| None`; `ActorSpawned.name: str \| None = None`. The goal is never sent to the client | The client must not show what Niko cannot know |
| Client | `web/src/game/extras.ts`: one body per non-player actor, a colour different from Niko's (a STYLEGUIDE token), Niko's depth function, the same per-tile visibility cutoff as tile objects. Positions interpolate from the previous to the latest tick over the measured tick interval (clamped 50–1500 ms). `MapScene` picks the player by `PLAYER_ID` only | At x1 an Extra moves up to 4 m per tick; without interpolation it teleports |
| Visual baselines | `POST /api/game/new` accepts `paused: bool = False`; `spawn.spec.ts` posts `{seed: 7, paused: true}`. The baselines are re-shot with the Extras at their start tiles and approved by the user | Moving bodies would make the seed-7 shots flaky |
| Event volume | 6 Extras, `actor.moved` per tile as today. Retention stays open in `PENDING.md` | Roughly 12 rows/s at x1 while they walk; the ~20-Extra block is where it must be decided |

## 3. What must not break

- Niko's movement, prediction and reconciliation: an Extra's `move` never reaches the player's
  `ack`/`result` stream (the hub only answers the socket that sent the input).
- The `test` world stays byte-identical (`test_world.py`) and its generator version stays 5; the
  `lab` version stays 1. Population is not part of `GeneratedWorld.blob_bytes()`.
- Opening an existing save emits nothing except the new `actor.spawned` rows when it had no
  Extras; a second open emits nothing.
- A paused clock: no brain run, no Extra moves (no `clock.ticked`).
- Every action of an Extra passes `handler.validate`; the brain has no private path to state.

## 5. Acceptance

1. Same seed twice gives the same 6 Extras (ids, names, tiles, anchors); each start tile is
   standable, walkable, reachable from spawn and ≥ 2 m from the others; `test` and `lab` both work.
   `server/tests/test_population.py`.
2. `ensure_world` on a save with only Niko adds the Extras without touching Niko, objects or
   chunks; a second open adds nothing. `server/tests/test_population.py`.
3. `0008_extra` upgrades a 0007 database in place and keeps `niko`. `server/tests/test_migration.py`.
4. `set_goal` refuses the player, an unknown actor and an unstandable tile, and logs
   `actor.goal_set` otherwise. `server/tests/test_extras_brain.py`.
5. Driving `advance_time` 120 times on seed 0: every Extra walks at least once and waits at least
   once; every `actor.moved` of an Extra is a legal step; no Extra leaves 12 m of its anchor
   (plus the path's detour). `server/tests/test_extras_brain.py`.
6. Two engines on the same seed driven 120 ticks produce identical event logs; an engine restarted
   at tick 60 on the same database continues the same goals and ends identical.
   `server/tests/test_extras_brain.py`.
7. An Extra shoved off its path recomputes it and still arrives; a goal walled in after it was set
   ends with `reason = "stuck"`. `server/tests/test_extras_brain.py`.
8. With the clock paused, 10 seconds of real time move no Extra. `server/tests/test_extras_brain.py`.
9. The menu on an Extra's tile offers `Inspect <name>`; adjacent, `Push/Hit <name>`; inspect text
   matches `Walking`/`Waiting`/`Standing`. `server/tests/test_ops.py`.
10. Interpolation: a position between two ticks is the linear mix by elapsed time, clamped at the
    ends; a first sighting snaps. `web/tests/actor-interpolation.test.ts`.
11. `npm run check` passes; `npm run check:visual` passes against the re-shot baselines.
12. Manual (a GUI is needed to judge motion): in the running game at x1, x3 and x10 the Extras
    walk smoothly near spawn, stop and wait, disappear with the cutaway like objects, can be
    inspected and pushed, and Niko still walks and predicts as before.

## TL;DR

Six seeded Extras (`kind = "extra"`, named, persisted with migration `0008_extra`) spawn near
Niko, walk and wait only through `submit`, keep a goal written by the engine (`set_goal`,
`actor.goal_set`), and follow a deterministic per-actor-minute routine run by a new
`server.minds` subscriber on `clock.ticked`. They walk at Niko's real-time speed, are drawn with
tick interpolation, and can be inspected, pushed and hit. Needs and utility come in Dev-019.
Major bump: v2.0.0.
